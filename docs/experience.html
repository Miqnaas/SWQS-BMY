06 / SIMULATED READINGS

Experiment with the sensor signals.

These controls adjust illustrative readings for learning purposes. They do not sample physical water, operate a real ESP32, or feed a validated nine-feature classifier.

Simulation ≠ prediction. Temperature is absent from the CSV; TDS is not automatically equivalent to its Solids feature; pH, turbidity and EC require calibration. The other five dataset measurements are not captured by this sensor kit.
