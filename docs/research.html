01 / RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS

Why monitor water quality?

Research sections 1–3 describe the problem, the proposed architecture, and the dataset used in the machine-learning experiment.

01

Research question

Can a reproducible machine-learning workflow estimate a dataset's potability label from nine water-quality indicators? How do we acknowledge the limits of that prediction?

02

Two separate paths

Offline CSV-based modeling is the evaluated path. A proposed Arduino/ESP32 sensor prototype collects fewer measurements and is not automatically compatible with the trained model.

03

Data & target

Each record contains nine numeric features and Potability: 0 = dataset label “not potable”; 1 = dataset label “potable”. The labels are not a certified safety determination.

View the nine feature definitions and proposed sensor compatibility ＋
